Twelve months with clear milestones

Your route from zero to B1 or B2.

The plan is adjusted after assessment and according to your time budget. B1 within twelve months is the steadier path. B2 requires the upper end of the hours and daily practice.

Two realistic routes: For B1, use months 10 to 12 for consolidation and exam preparation. For B2, follow the intensive route and reserve daily practice time. Mock-exam performance determines whether B2 after twelve months is sensible.

The sprint rhythm

Realign every
two weeks.

The monthly plan sets the direction. Each sprint turns it into concrete work for the next week.

  1. 01

    Set the target

    One skill and one measurable result for two weeks.

  2. 02

    Practise and apply

    Short daily sessions, one coaching meeting, and practical application.

  3. 03

    Review and adjust

    A short review shows what remains secure and what returns in the next sprint.
